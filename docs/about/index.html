Our Story

The human behind
the course.

Army Veteran. Business graduate. Entrepreneur. Not a financial advisor — just someone who refused to stay confused.

Kenzo
Founder, CryptoBasics

I'm not a financial advisor, and I'm not a Wall Street insider. I'm a veteran, a business graduate, and an entrepreneur who got tired of feeling like an outsider in a conversation that affects everyone.

Before I built this, I put in hundreds of hours of real diligence — technical interviews, analyst breakdowns, whitepapers, on-chain data. Not doomscrolling. Actual research, the same kind any serious investor does before putting money into anything. And even after all that, I still couldn't explain crypto to my sister in a way that made sense to her.

"Smart, educated people are getting left behind every day — not because they're foolish, but because nobody ever taught them the vocabulary."

That gap is exactly where confusion — and scammers — thrive. I decided not to leave it open.

My background isn't in finance — it's in business, sales, and marketing, with a stint studying abroad and four years in the U.S. Army working alongside military working dogs. Since then I've built things from nothing: sold everything I owned, lived out of a truck camper, and racked up 30K miles of wanderlusting around the country. I know what it takes to figure something out the hard way, and I know the discipline it takes to cut through noise and get to the actual truth of something.

We're living through a financial shift — crypto, AI, tokenization — moving faster than the internet did. You don't need a finance degree to keep up. You need someone to hand you the map.

"I built what I wish someone had built for me."

If you're here, you're already ahead of most people — not because you have more money or more time, but because you decided to actually understand what's happening. That's the whole thing. That's always been the whole thing.
